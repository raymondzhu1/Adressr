# Adressr
Adressr is a temporary name, but it's a practice web application for managing corporate employees as well as individual's careers. The current plan for it in the prototype phase is to offer features for both individuals and companies seeking management services.

Some of the features planned for now are included below:
* Manage your corporate structure employee wise by their roles in the company. You can move them up and down the corporate tree as well as add or remove employees from your company.
* Conveniently offers, at least those with permissions to, salaries, wages, benefits of those in your company. All viewable within one list, as well as having the ability to sort it from lowest to greatest.
* Offers a service to create your own interview questions and allow you to send it to individuals applying for a role to complete at their own pace.
* Add openings for your company that people can publicly can apply for.
* Manage the various openings within your company as well as keeping track of all the individuals who have applied for the position.
* A career manager feature that allows individuals to keep track of their career. They can add previous employments as well as current ones. In each of these records, it should have the position, role description, skills required, as well as the tenure.
  * Each employment can be made public or private by the individual, or just your entire employment history on this web application can just be turned to private or public.  
* A very simple analysis feature that can optionally analyze your career so far to give you advice on the next big move for you in your career.
* A place to add your current job that will list your position, roles, as well as current and previous tasks in our to-do list feature.
  * Removing said job from your current jobs can optionally add the inputted information for that role, even including your to-do list tasks, to your career manager automatically. Allowing you to automatically add more and more stuff to your career manager without having to manually add it every time.
* A public profile to show a summary of your professional entirety, as well as things to make an individual feel more human. Things such as maybe hobbies and interests, these interests would be related to your field of expertise in some way hopefully.


Future Features To Be Added:
* Ways to sort individuals within your company by specific roles while also sorting by other metrics like wages.
* Provide a screen share and a video call feature for the interview services, as well as offering it during individuals undergoing your interview exams.
* Offer a free resume builder that automatically creates a resume based off things you listed in the career manager.
* A more robust methodology that will analyze your career manager and offer you advice on what jobs could be the next best move, whether if it's to increase your skill set or move up to a better position.

Things that are a bit iffy:
* Adding a location for contracts to be added digitally per individual, so that contracts for each individual are managed in the system as well. Individuals would also be able to easily access their own contracts even after leaving the company.
